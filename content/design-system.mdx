## Tables

GFM pipes, mapped onto the shadcn primitive.

| Approach | Ships to the browser | Authoring |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GFM pipes | nothing, the plugin runs at build | a row per line |
| `<Table>` composed by hand | nothing, still a Server Component | JSX, for what pipes cannot say |
| TanStack Table | 31 kB, and `'use client'` | a column definition per table |

Alignment comes from the delimiter row.

| Left | Centre | Right |
| :--- | :----: | ----: |
| `--primary` | 262.04 | 1.0 |
| `--border` | 17 presets | 0.5 |

Past the measure, a table scrolls inside its own edge.

| Preset | Hue | Lightness | Light | Dark | Floor | Tailwind |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Blue | 259.8 | default | 4.06 | 7.21 | 3.79 | blue-500 |
| Violet | 292.7 | +0.02 | 3.91 | 6.88 | 3.79 | violet-500 |
